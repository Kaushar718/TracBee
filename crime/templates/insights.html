Growth Forecast

India’s digital economy projected to grow at 14.6% CAGR through FY27, driven by AI, cloud, and analytics.

  • Data Analytics: 17% CAGR; top demand in retail, healthcare, & finance.
  • Cybersecurity: $4.3B industry by 2027; 19% YoY hiring growth.
  • AI / Automation: +40,000 new roles by 2027 with Gen-AI expansion.
  • FinTech: 16% annual growth; Python & blockchain key skills.
  • Green Tech: +25% new hiring in smart manufacturing sectors.

Tier-2 cities to contribute over 20% of net new jobs as digital hubs decentralize.

Risk Assessment

Digital acceleration brings workforce volatility — automation, regulation, and talent clustering drive risk exposure.

  • Automation Risk: Routine roles down 35–45% by 2028.
  • Skill Decay: Legacy tech hiring -8% YoY as modernization accelerates.
  • Attrition: Data/AI roles see >22% annual churn due to global remote options.
  • Economic Headwinds: Global slowdown may trim 5% export IT hiring budgets.
  • Talent Clustering: 70% AI engineers in 3 metros = retention & cost challenges.

Mitigation: Continuous reskilling, hybrid-skill adoption, and internal mobility programs are key stabilizers.
